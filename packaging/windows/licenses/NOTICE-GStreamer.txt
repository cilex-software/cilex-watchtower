GStreamer — LGPL notice + shipped inventory (Windows distribution)
==================================================================

GStreamer is licensed under the GNU LGPL v2.1 (or, at your option, any later version).
Watchtower drives GStreamer directly (the live-camera decode path), so its runtime is
NOT handled by windeployqt and is bundled separately. We ship ONLY the LGPL subset the
pipeline needs — never gst-plugins-ugly or any GPL/patent-encumbered codec plugin.

Upstream + source: https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/  (version in SOURCES.md)
License text: LGPL-2.1 — https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
(Watchtower also ships LGPL-3.0.txt for Qt; GStreamer is "v2.1 or later".)

SHIPPED CORE/SUPPORT DLLs (dynamic link) — copied by packaging/windows/deploy.ps1:
  gstreamer-1.0, gstbase-1.0, gstapp-1.0, gstvideo-1.0, gstrtp-1.0, gstrtsp-1.0,
  gstsdp-1.0, gstnet-1.0, gstpbutils-1.0, gstaudio-1.0, gsttag-1.0, gstcodecparsers-1.0
  + GLib: glib-2.0, gobject-2.0, gmodule-2.0, gio-2.0, gthread-2.0
  + deps: ffi, intl, z-1, orc-0.4, graphene-1.0, libwinpthread, pcre2-8

SHIPPED PLUGINS (LGPL) under lib\gstreamer-1.0\  (gst<name>.dll):
  coreelements, playback, typefindfunctions, videoconvertscale, videoparsersbad,
  app, rtp, rtsp, rtpmanager, rtspclientsink, udp, tcp, autodetect, d3d11

  (d3d11 = the DXVA hardware decoder — the Windows equivalent of Linux VA-API. The
   H.264/H.265 PATENT obligation rides on the OS/GPU decoder, not on us; WT_HW_ONLY=1
   enforces hardware decode so no bundled software codec ever carries it.)

NOTE: this inventory is kept in lockstep with deploy.ps1's copy lists; the CI has a
drift check (a shipped plugin not listed here, or vice-versa, fails the build).
